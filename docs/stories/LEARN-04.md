# LEARN-04 — Pause and resume a Track

- **Epic:** Enrollment and progress
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; retirement interaction unresolved

## Story
As a learner, I want to leave and later resume an enrollment with its original requirements and history, so that a break does not erase my work.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Leaving archives the enrollment and ends mentoring.
- Former mentors lose access; pending submissions end without a decision and return to IN PROGRESS.
- History and approvals remain.
- Returning restores original requirements and progress, not the latest Track version.
- Mentor access is not restored automatically; a new mentoring connection is required.

## Boundaries
Leaving is not deletion or a fresh enrollment. Migration to updated requirements is deferred.

## Assumptions and open questions
**Assumption:** one continuing enrollment per learner per Track. Resuming a retired Track and visibility/actionability while archived need refinement.
