# MENTOR-01 — Approve mentors

- **Epic:** Mentoring relationships
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; eligibility details unresolved

## Story
As an administrator, I want to approve who may mentor, so that the pilot’s endorsements come from accepted mentors.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Administrator approval is required before a user may mentor.
- A person can be both learner and mentor.
- Approved mentors establish Track-scoped relationships and review assigned work.
- Only the active mentor reviews a learner’s enrollment; administrators cannot override decisions.

## Boundaries
Approval does not imply formal certification. No qualification tests, mentor matching, or per-Track credentialing process has been confirmed.

## Open questions
What makes a mentor eligible? Is approval global or Track-specific? How is approval requested and communicated? What happens if approval is revoked while relationships or reviews exist?
