# LEARN-02 — Organize my work

- **Epic:** Enrollment and progress
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; ready for product refinement

## Story
As a learner, I want to see requirements on a Kanban board and move work from waiting to in progress, so that I can plan and understand my progress.

## Relevant confirmed context
- All requirements begin in WAITING.
- The board presents WAITING, IN PROGRESS, IN REVIEW, and DONE.
- Learners begin work in IN PROGRESS, including without a mentor.
- IN REVIEW requires evidence-backed submission to an active mentor.
- DONE requires mentor approval; it is not a freely selectable learner status.

## Boundaries
Do not treat all status moves as unrestricted. Submission, review decisions, and withdrawal belong to their review stories. Sorting, dependencies, optional cards, and gamification are not confirmed.

## Open questions
Can work return from IN PROGRESS to WAITING? How should accidental moves be corrected? Mobile and accessibility expectations need refinement.
