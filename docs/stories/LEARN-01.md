# LEARN-01 — Choose my development goals

- **Epic:** Enrollment and progress
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; ready for product refinement

## Story
As a learner, I want to enroll directly in one or more Tracks, so that I can pursue the career goals relevant to me.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Tracks are independent target roles/levels with no earlier-level prerequisites.
- Multiple Track enrollments are allowed.
- Each enrollment has its own requirements and at most one active mentor.
- A mentor is not required to enroll or prepare work.
- Retired Tracks do not accept new enrollment.

## Boundaries
Do not add placement tests, administrator admission approval, mentor matching, or required sequencing.

## Assumptions and open questions
**Assumption:** one continuing enrollment per learner per Track, not parallel duplicates. How learners choose among available Tracks and how duplicate enrollment attempts behave need refinement.

- [LEARN-04](LEARN-04.md): return to an existing enrollment.
