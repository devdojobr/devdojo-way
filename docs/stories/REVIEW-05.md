# REVIEW-05 — Improve and resubmit

- **Epic:** Evidence and review
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; ready for product refinement

## Story
As a learner, I want to revise rejected work and submit another iteration, so that I can act on feedback and demonstrate improvement.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Rejection returns work to IN PROGRESS and includes an explanation.
- Learners can revise evidence and resubmit repeatedly.
- Each submission preserves its own evidence and review history rather than overwriting earlier attempts.
- Resubmission requires evidence and an active mentor.
- A replacement mentor sees history and reviews resubmitted work after a relationship ends.

## Boundaries
Do not introduce attempt limits, penalties, deadlines, or compulsory courses. Resubmitting approved work is not included because reopening is deferred.

## Open questions
How much change is necessary for a meaningful resubmission? No minimum-change rule is confirmed. How should learners connect revised evidence to previous feedback?
