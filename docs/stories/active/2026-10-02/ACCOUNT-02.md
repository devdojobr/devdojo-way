# ACCOUNT-02 — Obtain support or close my account

- **Epic:** Accounts and participation
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; launch policy unresolved

## Story
As a participant, I want administrator assistance with unwanted mentoring or account closure, so that I can control my participation.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Administrator-assisted support and account closure are included in the pilot.
- Either party may independently end mentoring; former mentors lose access immediately.
- Learners retain history when mentoring ends.
- Administrators cannot rewrite evidence or review decisions.

## Boundaries
Do not equate account closure with deletion or anonymization. A reporting/blocking system, automated moderation, and self-service deletion are not confirmed capabilities.

## Open questions
**Before launch:** What is removed or retained on closure? How is shared review history treated? What are participants told, and how can they contact support?
