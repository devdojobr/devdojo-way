# MENTOR-04 — Understand an assigned learner’s progress

- **Epic:** Mentoring relationships
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; ready for product refinement

## Story
As an active mentor, I want to inspect the assigned enrollment’s progress, evidence, and review history, so that I can give informed guidance.

## Relevant confirmed context
- A mentoring relationship covers one Track enrollment.
- The active mentor sees that enrollment’s progress, evidence, and reviews.
- Replacement mentors may see retained history from previous mentors.
- Other enrollments are not included merely because they belong to the same learner.
- Former mentors lose access immediately when the relationship ends.

## Boundaries
Mentor visibility does not permit editing learner evidence or prior decisions. Public profiles, broad learner browsing, and analytics dashboards are not confirmed.

## Open questions
Which minimal learner identity/profile details support mentoring? How should old and current iterations be distinguished? Viewing archived enrollments must remain consistent with ended access.
