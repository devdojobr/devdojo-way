# LEARN-05 — Recognize endorsed completion

- **Epic:** Enrollment and progress
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; correction policy unresolved

## Story
As a learner, I want my Track to become complete when all requirements are approved, so that I can clearly distinguish endorsed achievement from work still in progress.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Every requirement is mandatory.
- Completion follows approval of every requirement in the learner’s enrolled version.
- No separate final assessment or sign-off is required.
- Completion means mentor endorsement, not employment, promotion, or guaranteed overall readiness.
- Approvals remain when a mentor leaves or an enrollment is paused.

## Boundaries
No self-approved completion, certificates, public claims/profile features, or mandatory earlier Tracks. Reopening approved work is deferred.

## Open questions
**Before launch:** how to handle mistaken approvals while reopening and administrator overrides are excluded. Repeat completion and evidence reuse are not decided.
