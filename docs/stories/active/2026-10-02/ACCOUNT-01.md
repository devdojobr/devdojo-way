# ACCOUNT-01 — Access my account

- **Epic:** Accounts and participation
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; ready for product refinement

## Story
As a participant, I want to register, access, and recover my account, so that I can maintain continuity in my learning and mentoring.

## Relevant confirmed context
- The first release serves a focused developer-community pilot.
- Registration appears in the source idea; account access and recovery were confirmed in discovery.
- A participant may be both learner and mentor, but mentoring requires administrator approval.
- Enrollment progress is private by default.

## Boundaries
Do not assume an invited-only registration policy, public profiles, organization membership, or a specific sign-in technology. Account closure is a separate goal.

## Open questions
Registration eligibility and the minimum account/profile information are not decided. Recovery behavior needs product refinement without choosing its technical mechanism.

## Dependencies
- [MENTOR-01](MENTOR-01.md): mentor approval is distinct from account registration.
- [ACCOUNT-02](ACCOUNT-02.md): account closure and retained history.
