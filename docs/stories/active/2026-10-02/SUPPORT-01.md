# SUPPORT-01 — Resolve mentoring problems responsibly

- **Epic:** Support and evidence boundaries
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; support-access policy unresolved

## Story
As an administrator, I want recorded support access and the ability to end problematic relationships, so that I can help participants without rewriting their achievements.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Progress is private by default.
- Administrators may inspect enrollment history for support and end mentoring relationships.
- Support access/intervention is recorded.
- Administrators cannot change learner evidence, rewrite review decisions, or approve work as an administrator.
- Ending a relationship removes mentor access; history and approvals remain, while pending work returns to IN PROGRESS.

## Boundaries
Do not assume unrestricted routine monitoring, administrator review overrides, a moderation platform, or a technical audit architecture.

## Open questions
**Before launch:** what justifies inspection, what information/actions are recorded, who sees the record, and how participants request support. Is administrator-assisted support sufficient for the pilot?
