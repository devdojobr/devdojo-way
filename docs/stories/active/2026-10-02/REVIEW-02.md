# REVIEW-02 — Withdraw and revise a submission

- **Epic:** Evidence and review
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; withdrawal destination awaiting confirmation

## Story
As a learner, I want to withdraw pending work before revising its evidence, so that the mentor does not judge a changing submission and earlier iterations remain understandable.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Evidence in a submitted iteration is preserved unchanged.
- A learner must withdraw before revising evidence during pending review.
- Resubmission creates a new iteration; earlier evidence and feedback remain available.
- Approved work and decisions cannot be reopened in the MVP.

## Boundaries
Withdrawal does not erase the submission or rewrite a completed decision. Do not permit live editing of evidence currently under review.

## Assumptions and open questions
**Assumption:** withdrawal returns work to IN PROGRESS. Confirm this and how the withdrawn outcome is presented. What happens if withdrawal and a mentor decision occur at nearly the same time? How are obsolete alerts handled?
