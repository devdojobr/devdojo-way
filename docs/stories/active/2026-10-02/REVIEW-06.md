# REVIEW-06 — Understand the achievement history

- **Epic:** Evidence and review
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; deletion and comment policies unresolved

## Story
As a learner or active mentor, I want to inspect preserved submissions, feedback, reviewers, decisions, and timestamps, so that I can understand what was reviewed and how the work developed.

## Relevant confirmed context
- History retains submitted text/links, discussion, reviewer, decision/outcome, and timestamps across iterations.
- Submitted evidence remains unchanged; approved evidence and decisions remain unchanged.
- Relationship ending retains history and approvals for the learner.
- Pending work ended by relationship termination is recorded as ended without a decision.
- Replacement mentors see earlier history; former mentors lose access.
- Administrators may inspect history for recorded support but cannot rewrite evidence or decisions.

## Boundaries
Retained links do not archive external content. Do not infer unlimited retention, public history, exports, or a technical audit-log design.

## Open questions
**Before launch:** account deletion versus shared-history retention. Later: discussion editing/deletion, presentation of withdrawn iterations, and visibility of support records.
