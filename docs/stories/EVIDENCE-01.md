# EVIDENCE-01 — Share appropriate review evidence

- **Epic:** Support and evidence boundaries
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; pilot guidance needs refinement

## Story
As a learner, I want guidance on avoiding confidential material and providing accessible evidence, so that my work can be reviewed without inappropriate disclosure.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Evidence consists of text and links; uploads are deferred.
- Learners are warned against sharing confidential or employer-owned information inappropriately.
- When a mentor cannot inspect a link, they request accessible, shareable alternative evidence.
- Evidence is visible to the learner, the active Track mentor, and administrators providing recorded support.
- External link accessibility, safety, permanence, and unchanged content are not guaranteed.

## Boundaries
Do not silently require public evidence only. No automatic link scanning, external access provisioning, confidential-document storage, or link-content archiving is committed.

## Open questions
**Before launch:** does this guidance suffice for actual pilot participants? What should participants do if confidential material is submitted accidentally, given preserved submissions and unresolved deletion policy?
