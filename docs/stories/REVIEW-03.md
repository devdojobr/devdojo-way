# REVIEW-03 — Discuss a submission

- **Epic:** Evidence and review
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; discussion lifecycle unresolved

## Story
As a learner or mentor, I want to exchange comments within a review, so that we can clarify evidence and expectations without forcing an immediate decision.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Both participants may reply within the review.
- Comments alone do not change requirement status.
- Discussion is retained with review iterations.
- Replies notify the other participant in-app.
- Comments cannot be used to modify the submitted evidence; revision requires withdrawal or a subsequent iteration.

## Boundaries
This is review-specific discussion, not standalone chat or a social feed. Administrators cannot rewrite evidence or decisions.

## Open questions
Can discussion continue after a decision or withdrawal? Can comments be edited or deleted, and how would retention work? How is inappropriate feedback addressed through pilot support?
