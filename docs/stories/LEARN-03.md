# LEARN-03 — Prepare evidence independently

- **Epic:** Enrollment and progress
- **Scope:** Essential MVP candidate
- **Status:** High-level story; ready for product refinement

## Story
As a learner, I want to collect text and link evidence even without a mentor, so that I can prepare for a meaningful review.

## Relevant confirmed context
- Text and links are sufficient for the MVP; uploads are deferred.
- No mentor is needed to organize work or prepare evidence.
- Without a mentor, work remains IN PROGRESS rather than entering review or DONE.
- Submitted evidence is preserved per iteration; pending evidence must be withdrawn before revision.

## Boundaries
Independent preparation does not provide self-endorsed completion. Do not assume external integrations, automatic evidence assessment, link archiving, or file storage.

## Open questions
Draft evidence editing/removal and cross-Track evidence reuse need refinement. No automatic credit across Tracks has been confirmed.
